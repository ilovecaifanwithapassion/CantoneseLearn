Foundation

The Cantonese Landscape

Cantonese (粵語 Jyut6 jyu5) is a living classical language spoken by over 85 million people worldwide.

🗣️A Living Classical Language

Preserves pronunciation features from Tang Dynasty Middle Chinese (-p, -t, -k stops) disappeared from Mandarin.

85M+ Native Speakers Tang Poetry Rhymes Cantopop & Cinema

📜Traditional Characters (繁體字)

Hong Kong strictly preserves Traditional characters, keeping semantic radicals intact (愛 with 心, 聽 with 耳).

Essential Phrases

Master 唔該 vs 多謝, polite particles, introductions, and yes/no echoing.

唔該 (m4 goi1)

Service, Favors & Excuse Me

Used when someone opens a door, pours water, or to get someone's attention.

多謝 (do1 ze6)

Gifts, Compliments & Red Packets

Used for physical gifts, festive red packets (利是), and heartfelt compliments.
